# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that keeps this project workable:

- Judging a change on the code, not on who wrote it.
- Giving review feedback that names the problem and, where possible, the fix.
- Accepting that a maintainer may decline a change that does not fit the project's constraints.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or review.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when someone is
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close pull requests, or block accounts when this
document is broken.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
