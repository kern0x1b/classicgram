iOS 6.1.3 (10B329, iPhone4,1) - what the incoming-call path is actually made of.

Read out of the stock firmware, not off the device: the root filesystem of
iPhone4,1_6.1.3_10B329 mounted read-only, SpringBoard.app/SpringBoard parsed
directly and the frameworks parsed out of
System/Library/Caches/com.apple.dyld/dyld_shared_cache_armv7.

Findings that decided the design:

  * There is no SBCallAlert on this build. SpringBoard's only call classes are
    SBCallFailureAlert / SBCallFailureAlertItem / SBCallFailureAlertDisplay,
    SBCallPermissionAlertItem, SBAwayInCallController and SBTelephonyManager.

  * The incoming-call screen belongs to MobilePhone.app: InCallController and
    PhoneCall, and PhoneCall is a wrapper around a CTCallRef. SpringBoard drives
    it through the private URL schemes MobilePhone declares - telshow, tellock,
    telanswer, telemergency. A CTCallRef comes from CommCenter and nothing else,
    so that screen cannot be raised for a call the modem never saw.

  * TelephonyUtilities.framework exists on this build but is only logging plus
    TUReplyWithMessageStore. There is no TUCall or TUCallCenter - no CallKit
    ancestor to talk to.

  * What SpringBoard does own, and what its own call alert is built on, is
    SBAlertItem (SpringBoardUI.framework). SBCallFailureAlertItem is exactly an
    SBAlertItem with -lockLabel, -performUnlockAction and a two-button sheet.
    -[SBAwayView _updateLockBarLabelByClearingFirst:] and
    -[SBAwayBulletinCell _createLockBar] read -lockLabel;
    -[SBAwayController _finishUnlockWithSound:unlockSource:isAutoUnlock:] calls
    -performUnlockAction. That is the lock-screen answer affordance.

  * SpringBoard.strings carries SLIDE_TO_ANSWER ("slide to answer") in every
    lproj, and TelephonyUI.framework/*/General.strings carries ANSWER and
    DECLINE. The tweak takes its three visible strings from those two bundles.
